HOW IT WORKS

Pick. Style. Adjust. Share.

Four simple steps. No complicated editing.

01

Pick

Start with a photo from your library or take one on the spot. Switch between the full face and a closer eye view whenever you want.

02

Style

Pair lashes and lenses

Mix classic or volume lashes with different colored contacts to compare eye looks directly on your own photo.

03

Adjust

Fine-tune every detail

Refine density, length, and thickness. When needed, adjust the position and rotation of each side for a more natural finish.
